## 4. Seeds

Stage A (in the manifests): `P(D) = Φ(lean / σ)`, lean = mean of the 2020 and
2024 two-party margins relative to the national margin (FEC state tables; The
Downballot's exact district totals on 2026 lines, 2024 only where lines
changed), σ = 9 (president) / 10 (House) / 14 (Senate) / 20 (governor) points
for 2028, ×4/3 for 2032 and ×5/3 for 2036; "Another party or independent"
1% except eight reviewed Senate seats (AK 8%, UT 6% in 2028; AK 5%, ME 6%,
NE 10% in 2032; ME 10%, NE 5%, VT 10% in 2036). Full formula, parameters and
justifications: `SEEDS.md`.

Stage B (`market-seeds.ts`, re-runnable): Kalshi public prices with the 2026
matching and spread rules, keeping the reviewed "Another party" share when
Kalshi prices no independent; otherwise the prior blended with the certified
2026 results (`--results-2026`); coverage report; approval reset.

Kalshi coverage on 2026-10-06 (`seed-coverage-2026-10-06.md`): 2028 Senate
32 of 34 usable (missing AL and WA), 2028 governors 10 of 11 (missing NH:
Kalshi's `GOVPARTYNH-28` carries 2026 rules), national 2028 (D 58 / R 41–42)
and 2032 (D 46–52 / R 47–53); no 2028 state-level presidential, House or 2036
events yet. Several priors differ sharply from Kalshi (Arizona Senate prior
38% D vs Kalshi 88%; Vermont governor prior 94% D vs 43%; Ohio Senate 21% vs
46%), which is the expected gap between fundamentals and a market that knows
the candidates.
