## Confirmed (no change)

- **Senate count is 35**: 33 Class-2 races plus the FL and OH Class-3 specials. There are no other specials; five vacancies occurred in the 119th Congress and all were filled.
  - **OK:** No special. 26 O.S. 12-101(B) bars one for an even-year vacancy in a term ending the following January, and the ballot has a single "United States Senator" contest. Nov 3 elects the full term starting Jan 3, 2027. The statute also says the winner "shall be appointed … to fill the unexpired term", but that is an appointment, not a ballot contest.
  - **SC:** The Aug 11 / Aug 25 special primary only replaced the deceased nominee. Darline Graham serves "until January 3, 2027". Nov 3 elects the regular full term: Graham (R) v Andrews (D).
- **Governors: 36 states** — matches the list given.
- **Michigan governor:** Duggan dropped out in May 2026. The official listing has five tickets (D, R, L, UST, Green) and no independent.
- **Alaska Senate:** Peltola (D), Dan S. Sullivan (R, incumbent), Dan J. Sullivan Jr. (registered R), Heikes (R). Heikes moved up when Leslie withdrew Aug 31.
  - The primary sample ballot listed "Sullivan, Daniel J. Jr." with a blank party line and "Sullivan, Dan S. (Registered Republican) Incumbent".
  - I could not confirm the general-ballot format. The Alaska Division of Elections site is behind a captcha. **News sources only.**
- **Nebraska Senate** (official list, 9/11): Ricketts (R), Osborn ("By Petition"), Marvin (LMN), Richards (NE Working People), Conboy (America First). **No Democrat**: Burbank withdrew and the party left the line empty.
- **Idaho Senate** (news only): Roth (D) withdrew Aug 28 and his name is off the ballot; no Democratic replacement. The field is Risch (R), Achilles (I), Fleming (I), Loesby (L).
- **Montana Senate** (official): Alme (R), Bankhead (D), Bodnar (IND), Austin (L), plus a filed write-in. Daines withdrew at filing.
- **Maine Senate:** the Democrat is **Troy Jackson**, chosen by convention on 7/25 after Platner withdrew. The inventory is right.
- **Washington top-two:** all 10 districts are D v R and match the inventory. Write-ins are allowed if declared.
- **Mississippi:** federal races are decided by plurality. The Dec 1 "general runoff" applies only to judicial races.
- **No House special on Nov 3.** Specials already completed in 2026: CA-1 (Gallagher), CA-14 (Wahab), GA-13 (Blair), GA-14 (Fuller), NJ-11 (Mejia), TX-18 (Menefee).
- **New maps in effect:** TX, CA, NC, OH, UT, FL, TN, LA, AL (the 2023 SB 5 plan). **VA keeps its 2021 map** (the 2026 map was voided). NY keeps its 2024 map. MTS lists exactly these states as redrawn and shows MO on the 2022 map, so I found no mismatch with the atlas.
  - I did not check whether MTS's Alabama shapes are the 2023 plan rather than the 2024 court plan.
